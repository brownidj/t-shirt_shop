# Returns, Refunds and Cancellations Policy

**Last updated:** 13 October 2026

This policy applies to purchases from **topository**, the business name of
**David James Browning**, sole trader (ABN 16 205 166 811).

For questions about an order, contact us at
[info@tshirts.topository.org](mailto:info@tshirts.topository.org).

## Made-to-order products

Our garments are printed to order by our Australian production partner. Production
normally takes approximately **10 business days**. Delivery time is additional and
depends on the delivery address and carrier.

## Cancellations and changes

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change an order. We may be
able to help before the order enters production.

Once production has begun, an order cannot be cancelled or changed. This includes
changes to the selected garment, style, colour, size, quantity or delivery address.

## Change-of-mind returns and exchanges

Because each item is made to order, we do not offer returns, refunds or exchanges
for change of mind. This includes where:

- the shopper selected an incorrect size, colour, garment, style or quantity;
- the garment supplied matches the size, colour and other options ordered; or
- a shopper changes their mind about the design or product after production has
  begun.

Please check the product details, selected options and size guide carefully before
placing an order.

## Normal print and garment variation

Colours may look different on screens and can vary slightly between screens,
garments and print processes. Garment measurements may vary by up to 2 cm from the
published size guide.

For direct-to-garment printing, temporary pre-treatment or press marks can sometimes
appear. These are a normal part of the printing process and should disappear after
the first wash when the garment-care instructions are followed.

Minor variations of these kinds are not, by themselves, faults.

## Faulty, damaged or incorrect products

If an item is faulty, damaged, misprinted, or materially different from your order,
email [info@tshirts.topository.org](mailto:info@tshirts.topository.org) with:

- your order number;
- photographs clearly showing the issue; and
- a short description of the problem.

Please contact us within **14 days of receiving your order** where reasonably
possible. This helps us investigate and resolve the issue promptly. It is not a
deadline that limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

Please do not return an item until we provide return instructions. We may ask for
the item to be returned so that it can be assessed. If we confirm a fault, we will
provide the remedy required by law and reimburse reasonable return-postage costs
where appropriate.

## Australian Consumer Law

Nothing in this policy excludes, restricts or modifies any consumer guarantee,
right or remedy available under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot lawfully be
excluded, restricted or modified.

Where a product has a major failure, the shopper may be entitled to choose a refund
or replacement. Where a product has a minor failure, we may repair or replace it
within a reasonable time, or otherwise provide the remedy required by law.

## Order and delivery issues

If an order has not arrived, or the delivery details appear incorrect, please email
[info@tshirts.topository.org](mailto:info@tshirts.topository.org) promptly with
your order number. Delivery estimates are estimates only and may be affected by
production, supplier availability, carrier operations or circumstances outside our
reasonable control.

This policy operates alongside our Terms of Sale and Privacy Policy.
