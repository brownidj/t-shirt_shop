# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **topository**, the business name of **David James
Browning**, sole trader (ABN 16 205 166 811), handles personal information through
the topository T-shirts shop.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at
[info@tshirts.topository.org](mailto:info@tshirts.topository.org).

## 1. Personal information we collect

We collect personal information that you provide when you use the shop, including:

- account details: name, username, email address and password hash;
- delivery details: recipient name, delivery address and phone number where provided;
- order details: products, selected options, order number, order history and delivery
  status;
- payment-related details supplied through Stripe, such as payment status and Stripe
  customer or transaction identifiers; we do not receive or store full payment-card
  numbers;
- wish-list, basket and recently viewed product information;
- communications with us, including design requests, enquiries, fault reports and
  associated photographs; and
- marketing consent, where you choose to receive news, new designs and offers.

We may also collect technical information generated by use of the site, such as IP
address, browser details and server logs, and cookie information described below.

## 2. Why we collect and use information

We use personal information to:

- create and administer shopper accounts;
- process payments, orders, delivery and customer support;
- keep business, tax and transaction records;
- investigate faulty-product, delivery, security or fraud concerns;
- respond to design requests and other enquiries;
- improve the operation and security of the shop; and
- send marketing emails only where you have opted in, or where otherwise permitted
  by law.

You can withdraw marketing consent at any time by contacting us or using an
unsubscribe option in a marketing email. Withdrawing consent does not affect
transactional emails about an order or account.

## 3. Who we disclose information to

We disclose only the information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose. This
may include:

- **Stripe**, which provides hosted payment checkout and payment services;
- our Australian production and fulfilment partner, **Garment Printing Group**, so
  that it can produce and dispatch your order;
- delivery carriers, to deliver and track an order;
- service providers that host, maintain or secure the site; and
- government, regulators, advisers or others where required or authorised by law,
  or to protect our rights, property, customers or the public.

Stripe and other technology providers may process information outside Australia.
Where we disclose personal information overseas, we take reasonable steps to use
reputable providers and limit the information to what is needed for the service.

We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

The shop uses necessary cookies and sessions to make it work. These include cookies
for your shopping basket, recently viewed products and session state. Basket and
recently viewed-product cookies are normally retained for up to seven days.

You can disable cookies in your browser, but the basket, login and checkout features
may not work correctly. This policy will be updated before non-essential analytics
or advertising cookies are introduced.

## 5. How we hold and protect information

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal
information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, modification
or disclosure. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the
purposes above, including legal, tax, accounting, dispute-resolution and fraud
prevention requirements. We then delete or de-identify it where practicable.

## 6. Access and correction

You may ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you. Email
[info@tshirts.topository.org](mailto:info@tshirts.topository.org) with enough
information for us to identify you and the request. We may need to verify your
identity before responding.

If we cannot provide or correct information, we will explain why, subject to any
legal restrictions.

## 7. Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, email
[info@tshirts.topository.org](mailto:info@tshirts.topository.org) with the details.
We will investigate and respond within a reasonable time. If you remain dissatisfied,
you may be able to complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner
at [oaic.gov.au](https://www.oaic.gov.au/).

## 8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will be
published on the shop with its effective date.
